Privacy Policy – Arrow Out! (¡Flechas Fuera!)

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Arrow Out! ("the App"), published by DuckGames ("we"), handles information. / La versión en español está más abajo.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, location or any account. Your game progress (level, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions and approximate location derived from IP. Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is governed by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and regions with similar laws are asked for consent through Google's consent form (UMP). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. The only third party receiving data is Google (AdMob), as described above. Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS).

4. Data deletion

Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored progress. We hold no data on our servers.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
